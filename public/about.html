About Lashed By Tash

Lashed By Tash is a lash studio and training academy in Val de Vie, Paarl. Tash has been a lash artist since 2018, and before that worked in higher education. That mix still shapes everything we do: lash sets designed around your eyes and your lifestyle, a real focus on the health of your natural lashes, and patient, honest teaching for the artists we train.

A studio made for slowing down

Our studio is in the Polo Village Offices at Val de Vie. It's clean, calm and cosy, a place to lie back, close your eyes and switch off while we work.

We're appointment-only, so there's no waiting room rush and your appointment is unhurried from start to finish.

Our team

Lashed By Tash is a small team. Tash, our owner and educator, has been lashing since 2018 and leads every course. Jessica specialises in classic lash extensions, lash lifts and brow lamination, and Monique in hybrid and volume sets. Every one of us works to the same standard: careful mapping, healthy natural lashes and a calm, unhurried appointment.
