Overview

A lash lift curls your natural lashes from the base using gentle Elleebana products, then a tint adds depth and colour. It opens up the eyes and makes lashes look longer and fuller.

The Korean lash lift gives a more lifted, upright curl. Both are low-maintenance and grow out naturally.

Ideal for:

  • A natural, low-maintenance look
  • Straight or downward lashes
  • Sport, swimming and active lifestyles
  • A break from extensions

Your
appointment

Consultation

We check your lashes and choose the curl that suits your eyes.

Lift

Your lashes are gently set on a silicone shield and lifted with the lifting lotion.

Tint

A tint is applied for darker, more defined lashes.

Nourish and finish

Lashes are conditioned and brushed into shape.

Results & aftercare

Results last around six to eight weeks, as your lashes grow and naturally shed.

  • Keep lashes dry for 24 hours
  • Avoid mascara for 24 hours
  • No steam or saunas for 24 hours
  • Brush lashes into place each morning
  • Use a lash serum to keep them conditioned
  • Avoid rubbing your eyes
