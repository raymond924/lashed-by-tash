Lash goals

Tell us the look you're after and we'll match it to the right set. Not sure? Book a consultation and we'll guide you.

  1. 01Natural, everyday lashesLashes that look like your own, only better: defined, a little longer and easy to live with.
  2. 02Soft, fluffy fullnessMore density and softness than a classic set, without looking heavy.
  3. 03Bold, dramatic lashesA dark, dense lash line that makes a statement.
  4. 04A textured wet lookDefined, spiky lashes with a glossy finish.
  5. 05Lashes without extensionsLift and tint your own lashes for a natural curl that lasts for weeks.
  6. 06Defined, fuller browsBrows shaped and set to frame your face.
  7. 07Small or sensitive eyesA gentle, careful approach for eyes that need extra care.
  8. 08Becoming a lash artistLearn to lash with a qualified educator, in small groups or one-on-one.
