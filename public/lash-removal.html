Overview

We dissolve the adhesive with a gel cream remover and lift the extensions away, without tugging on your natural lashes.

Book a removal when you want a break from extensions, or before switching to a lash lift.

Ideal for:

  • A break from extensions
  • Switching to a lash lift
  • Extensions from another studio

Preparation

Your lower lashes are protected with under-eye pads.

Removal

Gel cream remover is applied to dissolve the adhesive, and the extensions are lifted away.
