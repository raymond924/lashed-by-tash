Overview

Hybrid lashes combine single extensions with lightweight volume fans across the lash line. The result is fuller and more textured than a classic set, with a slightly diffused, feathery finish.

Hybrids are a lovely choice if your natural lashes are a little sparse in places, or if you want more impact than classic while still looking soft.

Ideal for:

  • Wispy, textured lashes
  • Filling gaps in sparse lashes
  • Moving up from classic
  • Everyday wear with extra impact

Your
appointment

Consultation and mapping

We agree on the length, curl and density you want and map where classic and volume will sit.

Preparation

Cleansing, priming and under-eye pads, with you lying back comfortably.

Application

Classic extensions and handmade fans are placed lash by lash, building texture where your set needs it.

Finishing

A final check, a brush through and your aftercare guide.

Results & aftercare

A full, fluffy set from the first appointment. Book fills every one to three weeks depending on your natural lash cycle.

  • Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours
  • Avoid oil-based products around the eyes
  • Brush through daily with a clean spoolie
  • Cleanse your lashes gently every few days
  • Skip mascara on extensions
  • Book a professional removal rather than pulling them out
