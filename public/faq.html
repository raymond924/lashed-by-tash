FAQ

Booking, payment, fills and training: the questions we're asked most. Anything else, WhatsApp us on 071 560 1577.

Your
questions

How do I book?

Appointments are booked online through Booksy. We are appointment-only, so we cannot take walk-ins.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations or changes within 24 hours, and missed appointments, are charged 100% of the treatment price.

How can I pay?

We accept card payments. We do not accept cash.

Can I bring someone with me?

To keep the studio calm, please come on your own: we cannot accommodate guests or extra people.

How should I arrive for lash extensions?

Please arrive with clean lashes and no eye make-up.

When can I book a fill instead of a full set?

For a fill, more than about 40% of your set should still be in place; otherwise we may need to book a full set. Fills are priced by how long it has been since your last appointment.

How do you keep the studio hygienic?

All surfaces and tools are disinfected between clients.

Where are you?

Polo Village Offices, Block D107, Val de Vie Boulevard, Paarl.

Do you offer lash training?

Yes. Tash, our owner and educator, leads every course: classic, volume, a masterclass and a course bundle. See our training page for details.
